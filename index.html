AVAILABLE OFFLINE

If the ground starts moving.

General safety reminders only. Follow official local guidance.

  1. Move away from the slide path.Get clear of slopes, channels, and debris. Do not approach the edge to look.
  2. Warn others from a safe place.Do not cross moving debris or a flooded road.
  3. Call for help.In India, call 112 when it is safe to do so. Share your location.
  4. Wait for official instructions.Do not return to a slope or road until authorities say it is safe.
